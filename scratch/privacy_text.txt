Privacy Policy
			
 
		
	
			
				
					
		
				
				
									
Snowflakz
WHAT WE COLLECT
When you visit 
Snowflakz.com
, we automatically collect certain information about your device, including your web browser, IP address, time zone, and some cookies installed on your device. Additionally, as you browse the site, we collect information about the individual web pages or products you view, search terms that referred you to the site, and how you interact with the site. We refer to this automatically collected information as 
“Device Information.”
When you make a purchase or attempt to make a purchase through the site, we also collect information such as your name, billing and shipping address, payment information, email address, and phone number. This is referred to as 
“Order Information.”
COOKIES
Cookies are small files stored on your browser that help us enhance your user experience. We use both 
permanent and session cookies
. These do not contain any personally identifiable information.
We use cookies to:
Help remember and process items in your shopping cart
Understand and save your preferences for future visits
Collect aggregate data about traffic and site interactions to improve functionality
We may also use 
trusted third-party services
 (like Google Analytics) that track this information on our behalf.
LOG FILES
We use log files to track actions occurring on the website. These logs collect information like IP address, browser type, ISP, referring/exit pages, and timestamps.
SHARING OF PERSONAL INFORMATION
We may share personal information with our corporate entities and affiliates. These entities may use this information to market to you unless you opt out.
We may disclose your data to third parties to:
Provide access to our services
Comply with legal obligations
Enforce our Terms and Conditions
Prevent fraudulent or illegal activities
We do 
not
 sell or share your information for third-party marketing purposes without your consent.
We may also share information with law enforcement or legal authorities when required to protect our users or the public.
LINKS TO THIRD-PARTY SITES
Snowflakz.com may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and recommend reviewing their privacy policies.
SECURITY PRECAUTIONS
We implement stringent security measures to protect your data:
Secure servers
Encryption and firewall protection
Limited access to authorized personnel only
However, 
Snowflakz.com is not liable
 for data breaches due to events beyond our control, including force majeure situations like natural disasters, cyberattacks, or internet failures.
ADVERTISEMENTS
We may work with third-party advertisers who serve ads on Snowflakz.com. These companies may use anonymized data about your visits to show ads for goods and services of interest to you.
UNSUBSCRIBE / OPT-OUT
You may opt out of our marketing emails at any time by emailing us at 
info@snowflakz.com
. We will remove you from all future correspondence.
DATA RETENTION
We retain your order information unless and until you request its deletion. This allows us to maintain accurate business records and comply with legal obligations.
BEHAVIOURAL ADVERTISING
We may use your personal information to deliver targeted ads or marketing content tailored to your browsing and shopping behavior.
CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
Snowflakz.com reserves the right to modify this policy at any time. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Orders shipped internationally may be subject to import duties, taxes, and/or customs fees. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient and are not covered by Snowflakz.

We can only deliver to physical street addresses; P.O. boxes and APO/FPO addresses are not supported.
Some remote areas may incur additional delivery charges or longer delivery windows. If your location is affected, we will notify you via email.

Delivery instructions (e.g., leave with a neighbor or in a safe place) can be added during checkout to avoid missed deliveries.
